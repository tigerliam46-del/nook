How Nook works

Servers and channels

A server is a space for a group. Press + on the left to create one or join with an invite code. Inside, text channels (#) hold the conversation and voice channels (🔊) are for talking. Click the server name to see who is in it and how many are online.

Friends and direct messages

Click the Nook logo to go Home. Add friends by username, accept requests in Pending, then press Message to chat privately.

Voice

Click a voice channel to join. Your browser will ask for the microphone. Audio goes straight between people, up to 8 per channel.

Roles and settings

Owners and people with the right role can open Settings to rename the server, write a description, make roles with colours and powers, and give them to members.

Your profile

Click your name at the bottom left, then Edit profile to upload a picture, pick a colour and set a status and bio. Use Theme to switch the look.
